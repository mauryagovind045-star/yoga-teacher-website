Introduction

A practice rooted
in discipline.

I’m Akshata — a Goa-based yoga instructor and aspiring yoga therapist with over a decade of competitive and teaching experience. My journey began at the age of eleven, competing at National Yoga Championships across India, and has evolved into a deeper pursuit of yoga as a science of healing and transformation.

Currently pursuing my MSc in Yoga Therapy at S-VYASA University, Bangalore — one of India’s most respected institutions for yogic sciences — I bring athletic precision and therapeutic understanding to every class.

I teach across four leading schools in Goa, coach competitive yoga athletes — my students have won gold at State level — serve as a State-Level Judge for Yogasana Championships, and lead retreats and beach sessions across India.

Whether you’re a beginner finding your breath, an athlete refining your form, or someone seeking yoga for healing — my classes meet you exactly where you are.
